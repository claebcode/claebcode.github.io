About Me

I co-created Code Otter, a web development learning platform now used in real classes, which taught me full-stack development, project design, and long-term maintenance.

    When I'm not coding, I'm usually composing music or playing piano. I enjoy combining technical problem-solving with creative expression, and I try to bring that mindset into everything I build.
